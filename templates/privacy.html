Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not ask for your bank account or card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service for you: to sign you in, to save your expenses, and to show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on our servers and protected with reasonable technical safeguards, including hashed passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, and your data will be removed from active storage.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
